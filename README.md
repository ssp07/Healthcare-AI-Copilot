# 🏥 Healthcare AI Copilot

Healthcare AI Copilot is an intelligent healthcare analytics platform that helps patients and healthcare professionals understand medical reports through data visualization, biomarker tracking, health risk assessment, and AI-powered insights.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📋 Patient Health Dashboard
- Analyze individual patient health reports
- View latest medical metrics
- Track historical health data

### 📈 Biomarker Trend Analysis
- Monitor Glucose Levels
- Track Cholesterol Trends
- Analyze HDL & LDL Performance
- Monitor Vitamin D Levels
- View Hemoglobin Progression

### 🚨 Health Risk Assessment
- Identify abnormal biomarkers
- Generate patient risk scores
- Detect potential health concerns

### 🤖 AI Health Insights Engine
- Automated report interpretation
- Personalized health observations
- Explain abnormal values in simple language

### 👥 Population Health Analytics
- Compare patient metrics across populations
- Discover healthcare trends
- Generate healthcare
